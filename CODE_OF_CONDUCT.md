# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

I pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and respectful, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what's best for the project and its users.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Trolling, or deliberately disrupting discussions.
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please contact the maintainer privately. Reports will be handled as discreetly as possible.

You can also use GitHub's **Report content** option (the `···` menu) where available, or [report abuse to GitHub](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).

I'll review concerns and take appropriate action when needed.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.
